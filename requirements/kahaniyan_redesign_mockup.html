Arjun aur Chalak Bandar
Ek sundar jungle mein, Arjun naam ka ek sahasī bachcha rehta tha. Ek din, Arjun jungle mein khelte-khelte ek ajeeb ped ke paas pahuncha jahan ek chalak bandar baitha tha.

Bandar ne kaha, "Arre Arjun! Kya tum mujhse tez ho? Main tumhe ek paheli bata-ta hoon." Arjun muskuraya aur bola, "Zaroor, batao!"

Bandar ne kaha, "Wo kaun si cheez hai jo sabke paas hoti hai, par koi usse dekh nahin sakta?" Arjun ne thoda socha... aur phir bol utha — "Apni awaaz!"
